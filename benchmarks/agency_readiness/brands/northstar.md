# Northstar Sustainable Apparel

## Brand Voice

Must avoid playful tones in all customer-facing copy. Use a calm, confident voice.
Never make unsubstantiated sustainability claims.

## Product Specs

Recycled fabric content is 80 percent. Target audience is 25 to 40 years.

## Rejected Directions

Rejected aggressive discount-led campaign. Never lead with discounts.

## Historical Observations

The spring campaign achieved 4.2 percent conversion.
