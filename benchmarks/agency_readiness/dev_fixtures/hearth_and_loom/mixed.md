## Product specification

Our voice is tactile, unhurried, and rooted in craft.

First-time buyers aged 20-30 are the core audience for this launch.

- Primary colour: mustard-yellow.

The new office location is near the train station and a short walk from
the bus stop. Office hours run from nine to five, with a one-hour lunch
at midday. User feedback is collected through surveys and used to
prioritize the roadmap. Installation requires a Phillips-head
screwdriver and approximately twenty minutes. Quarterly results will be
presented at the all-hands meeting on the first Thursday. Orders are
processed within one business day and shipped with tracking information.
The product ships with a two-year limited warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. Customer success holds a weekly review of
open support tickets and feature requests. Software updates are
delivered automatically and require a restart to take effect. The API
documentation is published internally and updated with every release.
