## Prohibited vocabulary (3)

Do not use the word "fast-fashion home" in any customer-facing copy.

Training materials include videos, quick-reference guides, and live
webinars. What is the warranty period? It is two years from the date of
purchase. The specifications table lists dimensions, weight, power draw,
and operating temperature. The mobile application syncs across devices
using end-to-end encryption. Action items were assigned to engineering,
design, and customer success.
