## Prohibited vocabulary (3)

Do not use the word "fast-fashion home" in any customer-facing copy.

The maintenance schedule recommends inspection every six months for
optimal performance. Compatibility charts show which accessories work
with which model variant. The specifications table lists dimensions,
weight, power draw, and operating temperature. Action items were
assigned to engineering, design, and customer success. Training
materials include videos, quick-reference guides, and live webinars.
