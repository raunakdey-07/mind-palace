## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

The product ships with a two-year limited warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The technical datasheet lists every certified
component and its tolerance. Can I return an opened item? Yes, within
thirty days with the original receipt. The maintenance schedule
recommends inspection every six months for optimal performance. Action
items were assigned to engineering, design, and customer success.
Customer support is available by email and phone during business hours,
Monday to Friday.
