## Prohibited vocabulary (2)

Do not use the word "carbon-neutral" in any customer-facing copy.

The compliance program addresses data retention, audit trails, and
access controls. The technical datasheet lists every certified component
and its tolerance. Customer support is available by email and phone
during business hours, Monday to Friday. Can I return an opened item?
Yes, within thirty days with the original receipt. Orders are processed
within one business day and shipped with tracking information.
