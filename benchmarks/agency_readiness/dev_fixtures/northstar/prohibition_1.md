## Prohibited vocabulary (2)

Do not use the word "carbon-neutral" in any customer-facing copy.

Battery life depends on usage patterns and typically lasts a full day on
a single charge. How long does shipping take? Most orders arrive within
three to five business days. Orders are processed within one business
day and shipped with tracking information. Pricing varies by region and
volume, with discounts for educational and nonprofit customers. The
specifications table lists dimensions, weight, power draw, and operating
temperature.
