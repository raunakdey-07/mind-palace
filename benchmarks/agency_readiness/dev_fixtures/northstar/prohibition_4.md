## Prohibited vocabulary (5)

Do not use the word "guilt-free" in any customer-facing copy.

Customer support is available by email and phone during business hours,
Monday to Friday. Software updates are delivered automatically and
require a restart to take effect. Battery life depends on usage patterns
and typically lasts a full day on a single charge. Each configuration is
tested against the same quality standard before release. What is the
warranty period? It is two years from the date of purchase.
