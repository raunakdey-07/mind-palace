## Prohibited vocabulary (5)

Do not use the word "guilt-free" in any customer-facing copy.

The mobile application syncs across devices using end-to-end encryption.
Compatibility charts show which accessories work with which model
variant. Our product line spans entry-level, mid-range, and professional
configurations. What is the warranty period? It is two years from the
date of purchase. The team reviewed the quarterly roadmap and agreed on
three priority initiatives.
