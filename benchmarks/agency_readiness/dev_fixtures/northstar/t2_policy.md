## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

Software updates are delivered automatically and require a restart to
take effect. What is the warranty period? It is two years from the date
of purchase. How long does shipping take? Most orders arrive within
three to five business days. Accessibility features include
high-contrast mode, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader support.
Battery life depends on usage patterns and typically lasts a full day on
a single charge. Orders are processed within one business day and
shipped with tracking information.
