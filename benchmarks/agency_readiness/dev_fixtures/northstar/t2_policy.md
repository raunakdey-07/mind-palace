## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

Pricing varies by region and volume, with discounts for educational and
nonprofit customers. The team reviewed the quarterly roadmap and agreed
on three priority initiatives. Each configuration is tested against the
same quality standard before release. The product ships with a two-year
limited warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Action
items were assigned to engineering, design, and customer success.
Training materials include videos, quick-reference guides, and live
webinars.
