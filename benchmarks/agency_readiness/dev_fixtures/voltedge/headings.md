## Current guidance

Orders are processed within one business day and shipped with tracking
information. The API documentation is published internally and updated
with every release. The design system defines colour, spacing, and
typography for all product surfaces. The compliance program addresses
data retention, audit trails, and access controls. Employees may expense
reasonable meals while travelling on company business. Customer support
is available by email and phone during business hours, Monday to Friday.
Software updates are delivered automatically and require a restart to
take effect. Remote employees connect through the virtual private
network using multi-factor auth. The conference room has a whiteboard, a
projector, and two video cameras. The mobile application syncs across
devices using end-to-end encryption. User feedback is collected through
surveys and used to prioritize the roadmap. Office hours run from nine
to five, with a one-hour lunch at midday.
