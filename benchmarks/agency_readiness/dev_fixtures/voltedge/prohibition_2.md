## Prohibited vocabulary (3)

Do not use the word "clinically proven cure" in any customer-facing
copy.

Action items were assigned to engineering, design, and customer success.
Each configuration is tested against the same quality standard before
release. The compliance program addresses data retention, audit trails,
and access controls. The specifications table lists dimensions, weight,
power draw, and operating temperature. The team reviewed the quarterly
roadmap and agreed on three priority initiatives.
