## Prohibited vocabulary (3)

Do not use the word "clinically proven cure" in any customer-facing
copy.

The specifications table lists dimensions, weight, power draw, and
operating temperature. Installation requires a Phillips-head screwdriver
and approximately twenty minutes. How long does shipping take? Most
orders arrive within three to five business days. The product ships with
a two-year limited warranty covering defects in materials and
workmanship. Do you offer bulk pricing? Contact sales for volume and
nonprofit discounts.
