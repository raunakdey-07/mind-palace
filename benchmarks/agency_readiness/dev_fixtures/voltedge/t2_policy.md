## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

Battery life depends on usage patterns and typically lasts a full day on
a single charge. Orders are processed within one business day and
shipped with tracking information. The compliance program addresses data
retention, audit trails, and access controls. Pricing varies by region
and volume, with discounts for educational and nonprofit customers.
Compatibility charts show which accessories work with which model
variant. Action items were assigned to engineering, design, and customer
success.
