## Product specification

Our voice is tactile, unhurried, and rooted in craft.

First-time buyers aged 20-30 are the core audience for this launch.

- Primary colour: mustard-yellow.

Customer success holds a weekly review of open support tickets and
feature requests. Training materials include videos, quick-reference
guides, and live webinars. Employees may expense reasonable meals while
travelling on company business. Remote employees connect through the
virtual private network using multi-factor auth. User feedback is
collected through surveys and used to prioritize the roadmap. Orders are
processed within one business day and shipped with tracking information.
Warehouse staff perform cycle counts every Friday morning before
opening. Office hours run from nine to five, with a one-hour lunch at
midday. Returns are accepted within thirty days of purchase with
original packaging and receipt. The mobile application syncs across
devices using end-to-end encryption.
