## Prohibited vocabulary (1)

Do not use the word "hotel-grade luxury" in any customer-facing copy.

Software updates are delivered automatically and require a restart to
take effect. The product ships with a two-year limited warranty covering
defects in materials and workmanship. The maintenance schedule
recommends inspection every six months for optimal performance.
Accessibility features include high-contrast mode, keyboard navigation,
and screen-reader support. The next review is scheduled for the first
Tuesday of the following month.
