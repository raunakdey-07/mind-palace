## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

The specifications table lists dimensions, weight, power draw, and
operating temperature. How long does shipping take? Most orders arrive
within three to five business days. The maintenance schedule recommends
inspection every six months for optimal performance. Customer support is
available by email and phone during business hours, Monday to Friday.
Can I return an opened item? Yes, within thirty days with the original
receipt. Orders are processed within one business day and shipped with
tracking information.
