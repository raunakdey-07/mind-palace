## Prohibited vocabulary (1)

Do not use the word "curated" in any customer-facing copy.

The maintenance schedule recommends inspection every six months for
optimal performance. Do you offer bulk pricing? Contact sales for volume
and nonprofit discounts. How long does shipping take? Most orders arrive
within three to five business days. The specifications table lists
dimensions, weight, power draw, and operating temperature. Software
updates are delivered automatically and require a restart to take
effect.
