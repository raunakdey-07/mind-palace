## Approved specification 1

The material is 80% GRS-certified recycled polyester.

The maintenance schedule recommends inspection every six months for
optimal performance. The next review is scheduled for the first Tuesday
of the following month. The product ships with a two-year limited
warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. User feedback is
collected through surveys and used to prioritize the roadmap. Battery
life depends on usage patterns and typically lasts a full day on a
single charge.
