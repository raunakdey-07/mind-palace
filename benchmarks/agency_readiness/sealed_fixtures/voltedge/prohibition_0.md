## Prohibited vocabulary (1)

Do not use the word "effortless" in any customer-facing copy.

Battery life depends on usage patterns and typically lasts a full day on
a single charge. The technical datasheet lists every certified component
and its tolerance. Action items were assigned to engineering, design,
and customer success. Software updates are delivered automatically and
require a restart to take effect. Our product line spans entry-level,
mid-range, and professional configurations.
