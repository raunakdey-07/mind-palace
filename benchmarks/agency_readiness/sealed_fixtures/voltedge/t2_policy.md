## Current policy

Must never use the word "curated" in public copy.

Instagram is the primary channel for launches.

Can I return an opened item? Yes, within thirty days with the original
receipt. The next review is scheduled for the first Tuesday of the
following month. Compatibility charts show which accessories work with
which model variant. The technical datasheet lists every certified
component and its tolerance. Pricing varies by region and volume, with
discounts for educational and nonprofit customers. The team reviewed the
quarterly roadmap and agreed on three priority initiatives.
