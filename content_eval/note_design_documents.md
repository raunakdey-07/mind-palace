# Design Documents That Get Read

Design docs fail by being too long or too vague to disagree with.

## Structure

- **Problem** in two sentences — if it takes a page, the problem isn't understood yet
- **Options considered** including the do-nothing option, each with its strongest argument
- **Decision and evidence** — what was measured or reasoned, not just chosen
- **Revisit conditions** — what new fact would change the decision

## Length Discipline

One page forces prioritization. A design doc nobody finishes is equivalent to no design doc, except it creates the illusion of process.

## Mind Palace Application

The retrieval-strategy decision, the Neo4j deferral, and the context budget each followed this shape. The revisit conditions are what make them living decisions rather than tombstones.
