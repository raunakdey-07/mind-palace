# Writing Milestone Reports

Process note written while structuring this repository's own reports.

## Structure

Lead with what changed, then evidence (benchmarks with configurations), then decisions made or revised, then limitations. Decisions without evidence are opinions; mark them as such.

## Discipline

- Report superseded results as superseded, not deleted — the history of what was believed and why prevents re-litigating settled questions
- Every number needs a reproduction command
- Failures found and fixed belong in the report; hiding them hides the lesson
- The "remaining limitations" section is mandatory: a report claiming none is not credible

## Test

A reader should be able to decide from the report alone whether to adopt, defer, or reject the milestone's conclusions — without rerunning anything.
