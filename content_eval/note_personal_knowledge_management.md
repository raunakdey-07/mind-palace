# Personal Knowledge Management

The user-facing philosophy behind Mind Palace's product goals.

## Capture vs Retrieve

Most PKM effort goes into capture (note-taking systems, hierarchies, tags). Retrieval is where value materializes — a perfectly organized archive you cannot query is a graveyard. Systems should bias toward cheap capture plus strong retrieval rather than expensive upfront organization.

## Why Search Beats Structure

Folders force premature classification: every note has exactly one home. Tags help but decay without discipline. Semantic retrieval sidesteps both — the note's content is its index.

## The Portfolio Angle

A portfolio is a PKM corpus with an audience. The same retrieval that answers your own questions ("what did I learn from X?") powers interview prep and resume evidence — one corpus, multiple consumers.
