# Reading Research Papers Efficiently

Method note behind the paper-notes corpus.

## Three-Pass Method

1. **Skim** (5 min): title, abstract, figures, conclusion — decide whether to continue
2. **Read** (30–60 min): method and experiments, skip proofs; note what would change in my own systems
3. **Deep** (hours): re-derive key equations; only for papers I will build on

## Personal Index

Every paper note records one extra section the original lacks: *relevance to my systems*. A paper without an application hook is filed but not retained. This is why the notes here reference specific projects rather than summarizing abstractly.
