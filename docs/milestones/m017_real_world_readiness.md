# Milestone 17 — Real-World Organizational Readiness (v0.9.3)

**Status:** In progress
**Target release:** v0.9.3
**Tracking issue:** #51

## Mission shift

Mind Palace helps AI applications use the right organizational knowledge at the
right time, while preserving where that knowledge came from, which version is
authoritative, and how it has changed.

## Engineering tracks

1. Doc Reconciliation & Reality Audit
2. Prose & Multi-Format Ingestion with Ingestion Coverage Validation
3. Adversarial Supersession Lineage & Explicit Authority States
4. Contextual & Example-Preserving Task-Aware Retrieval
5. Multi-Tenant Scope Token Hardening
6. End-to-End 4-Way Multi-Brand Evaluation Benchmark

## Honest boundaries

- Deterministic, CPU-friendly extraction by default; optional pluggable LLM provider.
- Exact-evidence contract preserved: every claim carries a verbatim source span.
- No heavyweight local LLM required for ingestion or tests.
