        inherited code, unchanged by the diff, and not a CI signal in this repo.

            path: Optional[str] = None,
        `from typing import Optional` is HEAD line 11 as well. Modernising this
        is a pyupgrade/UP007 concern, again ruff-only, again not in CI.

  460  "blind Exception at 460"

        _refresh_planner_statistics (ingestion.py:460). This is the one item in
        the list that is genuinely the change's own, and it must be said
        plainly rather than folded in with the other three.

        It is nonetheless NOT a CI failure and not a flake8 diagnostic:
        pycodestyle's E722 covers a *bare* `except:`, not `except Exception:`,

        so any failure to ANALYZE must be swallowed rather than fail an
        otherwise successful ingest. Narrowing it would re-introduce the bug the
        change exists to prevent. The bare `except Exception` at HEAD:260 carries
        the identical noqa comment for the identical reason.
